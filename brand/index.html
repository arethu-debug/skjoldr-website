03Core story Decided

The missing link in battling wildfires

Our narrative in three paragraphs: the problem, our answer, and where we are going. From the brand platform.

Across the world, wildfires are spreading faster and burning fiercer. Climate change, prolonged droughts, and global instability are fueling a new kind of existential threat. Investments in firefighting are rising, yet a crucial gap remains: the first phase of response. When fire breaks out, few are close enough, ready enough, or equipped enough to act in time. Making it the missing link in battling wildfires, that brief window where small fires turn into disasters.

Skjoldr was created to close a critical gap: the lack of tools for early, local response. Our modular fire suppression systems make rapid action possible anywhere and by anyone. With minimal training, our technology turns landowners, farmers, and local communities into capable first responders, restoring control to those closest to the risk.

In the coming years, Skjoldr will grow into a global force for early wildfire response. Our goal is to make first-phase readiness part of every safety infrastructure, ensuring that no community faces fire unprepared. We will expand through partnerships, innovation and responsible production, combining Scandinavian engineering with an international mindset. By turning early action into a shared standard, we will help protect lives, property and ecosystems, moving towards a world without wildfires.

04Vision, values, ambition, promises Decided

What we stand for

The values guide our decisions, our designs, how we grow the team and how we work together.

Effective

We don't wait for danger to grow. We stay alert, prepared and proactive, because readiness saves what matters the most.

Quick, fast, light, agile, simple

Local

We build strength by leaning on each other. Farmers, volunteers, firefighters and engineers, together we turn locals into first responders.

Community, together, collaboration, ownership, empower

Resourceful

We are in the business of solving challenges, and every obstacle is an opportunity to improve. That is why we question what's accepted and keep designing what's needed.

Curious, creative, forward-thinking, problem-solving

Ambition
Stopping wildfires before they become wild.

Our work starts where wildfires begin, in the moments when they can still be stopped. By building mobile response rigs for early readiness, we give people and communities the power to act before disaster spreads. We measure our success by two figures:

Mobile response rigs deliveredNumber per year
Fires put out before they became full-scale wildfiresNumber per year
Users

From bystander to responder

We empower people to act when it matters. With simple, mobile tools and basic training, anyone can make a difference in the first, crucial phase of a wildfire.

Public customers

The missing link in wildfire response

We strengthen existing systems with speed, mobility and accessibility. Skjoldr adds the first phase of response, helping public authorities contain fires before they escalate.

Private customers

Effective response at an affordable price

We deliver professional capability without complexity or high cost. Our modular systems make readiness realistic for private companies, landowners and industries that depend on safety and continuity.

Team

Empower the people that protect our communities

When you join Skjoldr, you join the world's first line of defense, enabling others to stop wildfires before they become wild.

Society

A world without wildfires

Our ultimate promise is to the world we share. We exist to protect communities and preserve nature, building a future where wildfire no longer threatens our daily life.

DNA
The First Response

At Skjoldr, the first response is more than just a phase, it's our core identity. It defines how we think, design and act. We know that the most powerful way to stop wildfires is to take action before disaster takes hold. That's why every idea, every solution and every rig we build is designed to empower others to become first responders. Because readiness without response changes nothing.

05Position Decided

Škoda+: quality costs a bit more

Above the low-price and purely functional end, without premium for premium's sake. High quality, strong value and clear character, with no unnecessary cost or complexity.

Best no matter the price
Quality costs a bit moreSkjoldr
Value for money
Lowest possible price

Skjoldr makes equipment for first responders who need it to work the first time, the second time, and every time after. That sets a high bar for quality, reliability and performance, and it means avoiding more technology, features or complexity than the job requires.

In the civilian market we build and support a complete product, ready to use out of the box, like a Škoda. The military market places higher demands, which is the + in Škoda+: there Skjoldr becomes a capability within someone else's vehicles, platforms and missions.

A brand is a promise, and the delivery on that promise, at an acceptable price, to both buyer and seller.

Ideology

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

We focus on value, not low price, and spend where it makes a real difference to quality, performance, reliability or use. We want the right product, not the cheapest or the most advanced one.

Production

One platform. Many jobs.

We build around a common platform rather than a new product for every need. For military use, the same platform steps up; what we add depends on the job.

Brand

Recognisable as Skjoldr

Professional equipment does not have to be anonymous. Function comes first, but design matters, from the products to the visual identity.

Culture

Shoulder to shoulder

We listen to customers, users, partners and people who know more than we do. We are straightforward about what we know and what we don't, and do what we say we will do.

06Visual keywords Decided Photos to come

Six words for every visual choice

The three values translated into six words that guide photography, communication, environments and industrial design. The image areas are placeholders until we have our own field photos.

Photo to come

Solid Effective

Things should look like they can take a hit. Weight, stability, substantial forms and strong construction. Never delicate or ornamental.

Photo to come

Earned Resourceful

Show the evidence of doing something rather than preparing to do it. Sweat, dirt, scratched equipment, used gloves, tired faces and wet clothing. After, not before.

Photo to come

Rugged Effective

Masculine without becoming macho. Functional clothing, rough environments, imperfect surfaces, physical work, weather and machinery. Strength without the theatre.

Photo to come

Tactile Resourceful

Materials should feel physical. Matt surfaces, visible grain, metal, rubber, fabric, dirt, skin, water and smoke. Texture over polish.

Photo to come

Confrontable Local

Fire should feel real and come with consequences, but never look beyond control. We show the scale of fire that makes first response matter: something that can be fought before it becomes something bigger.

Photo to come

Together Local

People doing the job together rather than individual heroes posing for the camera. Shared effort, proximity, trust, and hands helping hands.
